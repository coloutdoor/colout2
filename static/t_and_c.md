# Columbia Outdoor — Terms and Conditions

---

These Terms and Conditions ("Terms") apply to all estimates, proposals, and work orders ("Estimate") provided by Columbia Outdoor ("Columbia Outdoor") to the property owner ("Homeowner"). By accepting the Estimate (verbally, in writing, or by allowing work to begin), Homeowner agrees to these Terms. The Estimate, these Terms, and any signed Work Order form the full agreement.

## 1. Scope of Work

- Work includes only what is explicitly listed in the Estimate.
- **Any change to scope, materials, price, or schedule requires a written Change Order signed by both parties before proceeding — including zero-cost or reduction changes.** No verbal changes are binding.
- Columbia Outdoor handles permits, inspections, and compliance with local codes only if that option has been selected in the Estimate.
- Columbia Outdoor coordinates subcontractors as needed for project completion.
- **Service Area:** Columbia Outdoor provides services in Clark and Cowlitz Counties, Washington only.

## 2. Pricing & Payment

- Estimate is valid for 30 days from issuance.
- Pricing is fixed unless changed via a signed Change Order.
- **Price Escalation:** If a documented increase in material costs of 5% or more occurs between Estimate acceptance and material purchase, Columbia Outdoor may issue a Change Order reflecting the increase before proceeding with the affected purchase.
- **Payment Schedule:**
  - 10% deposit upon acceptance (non-refundable — covers planning, design, and permit costs)
  - Additional deposit collected prior to material purchase, such that **total deposit reaches a minimum of 50%** before any materials are ordered
  - Progress payments at defined milestones (e.g., framing complete, decking installed) for larger projects, released upon Phase QA approval
  - Balance due upon substantial completion and final walkthrough sign-off
- Late payments accrue 1.5% monthly interest (18% APR).
- All payments by check, ACH, or wire (no cash).
- No final payment until all lien releases are provided.

## 3. Timeline & Delays

- Estimated start/completion dates are approximate and non-binding until the Work Agreement is signed.
- Delays may occur due to weather, material delivery, inspections, changes requested by Homeowner, subcontractor availability, or unforeseen site conditions (e.g., soil issues).
- No liquidated damages for delays unless agreed in writing.
- Columbia Outdoor will communicate schedule changes promptly.

## 4. Permits, Codes & Inspections

- Columbia Outdoor obtains all required permits and schedules inspections when that option is selected in the Estimate.
- **If the permit option is not selected and the scope of work requires a permit under local code, Homeowner must sign a Permit Waiver acknowledging that (a) Homeowner is proceeding without Columbia Outdoor-obtained permits, (b) Homeowner assumes responsibility for any code compliance or permitting issues that result, and (c) Columbia Outdoor is held harmless for work performed without permits at Homeowner's direction.** This waiver must be signed before work begins.
- Homeowner grants access for inspectors and provides site access/utilities.
- Homeowner is responsible for HOA or landlord approvals, if applicable.
